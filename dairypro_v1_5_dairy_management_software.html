Enterprise Capabilities

Built Specifically for Dairies & Milk Centers

Everything you need to automate weighing, billing, inventory, and messaging in one lightning-fast Windows software.

🥛

Smart Milk Collection

Track morning and evening shifts seamlessly, auto-calculate total payouts based on Liters and Fat %, and manage customer rates effortlessly.

Android SMS Gateway Bridge

Connect your Android phone via local Wi-Fi/TCP gateway to automatically dispatch collection and statement text messages to farmers instantly.

Invisible WhatsApp Automation

Send rich-formatted account statements, shift summaries, and payment receipts directly via WhatsApp Web integration without manual typing.

Complete Account Ledger

Track milk credits (Cr), cattle feed or goods taken/debits (Dr), cash payments, and real-time net balances (Payable/Receivable) per farmer.

Product & Inventory Sales

Manage retail or supplier items such as cattle feed, medicine, and supplements with stock alerts and quick POS billing deductions.

Professional Reports & Printing

Print daily collection sheets, customer registries, and detailed ledger statements with custom date filters for transparent accounting.

Simple Setup

Get Started in 3 Easy Steps

Deploy DairyPro at your collection center in under 5 minutes with zero technical hassle.

1

Install on Windows PC

Download the lightweight DairyPro v1.5 setup package and run the installer on your Windows desktop or collection counter laptop.

2

Register & Log Daily Entries

Add your milk suppliers/buyers, set default rate charts, and log morning and evening milk quantities with Fat and SNF grades instantly.

3

Connect Phone & Automate

Link your Android phone via local Wi-Fi for automated SMS delivery or connect WhatsApp Web to dispatch collection receipts seamlessly.
